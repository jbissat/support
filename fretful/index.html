Fretful cannot hear my guitar

  • In your device's Settings, open Privacy & Security → Microphone and enable Fretful.
  • Use a six-string guitar in standard tuning. Play one note at a time, including chord tones; the app does not recognize a whole strummed chord.
  • Try a quieter room and move your device closer to the guitar.
  • In Fretful → Settings → Audio Input, check the selected input and channel if you use a compatible audio interface.
  • In Fretful → Settings → Pitch Detection, try lowering the Amplitude Threshold for quieter notes. Raising it can help reject background noise.

Purchases and Fretful Plus

Note Finder, triads, and shell voicings are free. Fretful Plus is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks Interval Builder, Drop 2 and Drop 3 voicings, shell voicings with extensions, and extended chord qualities. It is not a subscription.

To restore Plus, use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Fretful → Settings → Restore Purchases. An internet connection may be needed. If a purchase is pending approval, wait for approval before trying again.

If purchases are unavailable, check your connection and try again later. For refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem website.

TestFlight purchases are test transactions and do not charge real money. They do not grant ownership in the App Store release.

Practice history and privacy

Practice history is stored on your device. To delete it, open Stats → Clear All Data and confirm Delete All Sessions. This clears practice history and streaks, not preferences or Apple purchases.

Fretful does not provide its own account system or cross-device history sync. See the privacy policy for details about audio, storage, purchases, and support emails.
