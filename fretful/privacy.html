Privacy Policy

Fretful processes your playing on your device. You do not need a Fretful account to practice.

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Microphone and audio input

Fretful requests microphone permission to detect notes played on your guitar, using the built-in microphone or a compatible connected audio interface. Audio is processed in memory on your device for pitch detection and feedback, and is never recorded or written to storage.

Fretful does not upload microphone audio to the developer or to an audio-processing service.

You can revoke microphone access in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Listening exercises will not work without permission.

Practice history and preferences

Fretful stores practice records locally on your device. Records include session dates, exercise types, question prompts, correct or incorrect results, accuracy, and response times when applicable. They are used to show your history, accuracy charts, and practice streaks.

Preferences such as appearance, note naming, handedness, session settings, sound, and audio-input selection are also stored locally. Fretful does not upload practice history or preferences to the developer and does not provide its own cloud-sync service.

Depending on your device settings, local app data may be included in backups managed by Apple. Backup storage and restoration are governed by your device settings and Apple's policies.

In-app purchases

Fretful Plus purchases and restorations are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified purchase information to determine whether Plus is unlocked. These operations may communicate with Apple's services.

The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple may provide the developer with sales reports and information available through App Store Connect. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's handling of purchase and account information.

Analytics, advertising, and diagnostics

Fretful does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. It does not sell your personal information.

Apple may collect and share app usage, crash reports, or diagnostics according to Apple's services and your sharing settings. If you use TestFlight, Apple may make testing activity, crash reports, and feedback you submit available to the developer. These platform services are separate from Fretful's on-device audio processing.

Contacting support

If you email tabnit.dev@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot problems.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail and may be retained while needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any legal retention requirements. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Email processing is also subject to Google's privacy policy.

This support website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They do not include analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or embedded third-party content. The website does not set its own cookies.

GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser request details when serving the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement. Opening links to Apple, Google, or other sites is subject to their respective policies.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete practice history in Fretful → Stats → Clear All Data → Delete All Sessions. This resets history and streaks, but not preferences or purchases.
  • Delete the app, rather than offloading it, to remove its local data and preferences from that device. Existing device backups may still contain earlier data; manage those through your device's backup settings.
  • Manage microphone permission and diagnostic sharing in your device's Settings.
  • Manage purchases and refund requests through Apple. Deleting local data does not erase Apple's purchase records.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when Fretful's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with the revised policy.

For privacy questions or requests, contact tabnit.dev@gmail.com.
